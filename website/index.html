Overview

Who we are

Line Empower Services (PTY) LTD ("LES") is a Level 1 BBBEE contributor with 100% black woman ownership. Formed in 2015 and operational from January 2016, the owners saw an opportunity in the manufacturing of chemicals. We manufacture and distribute cleaning detergents for household, automotive (car wash) and industrial consumption.

We address the demand for essential everyday cleaning products used by every household. By manufacturing on-site, we minimise costs and increase productivity through high-volume sales at very low costs per unit. Our distribution point is a factory shop in Chiawelo, Soweto.

LES is committed to tackling unemployment — growing the business through more distribution channels across Soweto and surrounding areas, and exploring ownership of car wash outlets as part of our expansion.
